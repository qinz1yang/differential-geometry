The projected version uses actual continuous linear coordinate projection Q and inclusion J, with their contraction bounds, and the actual map P. AdjustmentProjections proves preservation of a killed complementary coordinate and the actual factorization over a retained larger coordinate map. It proves inclusion of the original derivative kernel under differentiable postcomposition, and equality when the new differential is injective on the original derivative's range. SubspaceRankTransfer treats the rank step when the original and perturbed images lie in different subspaces of the same ambient normed space. A perturbation smaller than the lower bound on the original kernel complement is injective on that complement. Rank-nullity and the stated finite dimension of the new target then prove surjectivity onto that actual target submodule. The target dimension is bounded by the ORIGINAL operator's range dimension. This prevents a zero operator's vacuous complement bound from producing surjectivity to a positive-dimensional target. The source is a finite-dimensional real inner product space; the ambient target need not be finite dimensional. The actual smooth tangent submodule and its dimension must still be supplied by the consumer.
