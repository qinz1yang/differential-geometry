    min(||mu||, ||mu-1||) ||v|| <= ||mu v - P v||.

Perturbation by an actual operator A gives the same bound with its error subtracted. Consequently every eigenvalue of A is within epsilon of 0 or 1 when ||A-P||<=epsilon; no self-adjointness is needed for this conclusion. In finite dimension the actual spectrum of the continuous linear operator is contained in the union of the two closed balls. A circle centered at 1 of radius r stays at least min(r,1-r)-epsilon from that spectrum. The module also gives the two quantitative eigenvector component bounds.
