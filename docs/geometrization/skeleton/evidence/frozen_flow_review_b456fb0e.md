## Same-flow physical area, with the remaining interface gap explicit

`hasExteriorAreaObstructionAfter` starts from failure of injectivity of the exact reconstructed seam map at any torus basepoint. Its exterior W(t), boundary loop γ(t) and disks all live in `postStage F.observation t`, and area is measured with that same flow's physical `postMetric`, not t⁻¹g(t). The existing observation restriction includes an event at time t, so this is the post-surgery convention. The total definition's negative-time clipping is irrelevant on T≥0.

A minimizing admissible exterior disk is required at every t≥T. Therefore the real infimum of an empty admissible family is not used to fake disk existence. The scalar least-area function must be continuous on the whole half-line and admit strict local smooth upper barriers at every time, including surgery contacts. The coefficient is 3A/[4(t+c)]−π with c>0. This shift accommodates arbitrary initial metrics; deriving the source estimate after the required normalization is part of the admitted producer, not a claim that the source's normalized c=1/4 applies unchanged.

The scalar contradiction only uses continuity, nonnegativity and the barriers. Projecting away the stronger physical-minimum and T≥t₀ evidence in this consumer is legitimate. However, the obstruction interface does **not** separately export the primitive meridian class, the identification of γ with the failed seam's persistent cusp family, or the common unchanged compact carrier and its transport maps. It expresses the weaker physical obstruction consequence needed for the endpoint. IMS01–IMS09 must still construct those geometric data to prove it. This is not advertised as a complete separate interface for surgery avoidance or meridian persistence.
