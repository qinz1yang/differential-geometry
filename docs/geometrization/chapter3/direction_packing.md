# Actual direction packings, original struts and local openness

Five public theorems in four leaves implement finite actual-direction packing to original common-radius struts, persistence of those same anchors, the reverse comparison-to-direction transfer at original anchors, and openness of actual direction packing in the controlled half-ball. No numerical rank convention, tangent existence or dimension equivalence is assumed or concluded.

## Exact mathematical contracts

For any two original positive geodesic representatives at a point with actual defined angles, the joint two-parameter comparison-angle limit for every κ≥0 equals their actual completed-direction distance. The proof adds the full curvature discrepancy limit to the canonical joint zero-curvature limit.

A supplied finite family of actual completed directions with all distinct labels separated by more than π/2, together with ε,S>0, yields one original representative per label, within ε in direction distance, and ONE common 0<s<S inside every original domain. A positive margin θ is chosen first; representative directions are separated by more than π/2+6θ and their actual endpoints at s have comparison angles greater than π/2+5θ. Exact original endpoint distances equal s. For κ=1, AFTER these same representatives, s and θ are fixed, every positive cap gives a neighborhood with all original anchor distances greater than s/2 and comparison margins greater than π/2+4θ. No Euclidean-tangent isometry, completeness or curvature hypothesis is needed beyond actual defined angles in this forward construction.

Conversely, complete X, arbitrarily short curves, local compactness of open ball(p,8R), intrinsic local curvature−κ comparison there, q in ball(p,R/2), and any indexed family of noncentral anchors in ball(p,R) give one actual family of original representatives. Their lengths and endpoints are exact; their full-real clamped paths lie in ball(p,2R). For every pair of labels, including equal labels or repeated anchors, the original endpoint comparison angle is at most the actual completed-direction distance. The index type may be infinite. No geodesic or endpoint-direction uniqueness is assumed. Unlike the source's general straight-point perturbation proof, the accepted local compact-buffer theorem directly joins the original anchors; no perturbation is hidden.

Combining both implications proves that the subset of ball(p,R/2) admitting n actual completed directions with strict π/2 pairwise separation is open in its actual subtype metric, for EVERY natural n. The SAME finite anchors persist while the center varies. Nearby representatives can be chosen independently at each center; their continuity or uniqueness is unnecessary. This is relative openness under the original intrinsic 8R geometry, not an unqualified global rank theorem.

The empty-index case never creates a direction. A singleton-index family requires an actual supplied direction. On a singleton source, a one-anchor comparison condition alone is vacuous and cannot imply a direction; reverse hypotheses explicitly exclude central anchors. This boundary is part of the contract and tests.
