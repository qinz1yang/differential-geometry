# Compact neighborhoods at every prescribed point

Two public theorems apply AC32 finite transport and the AC28 compact chart
patch to every prescribed point of an open common comparison domain.
The assumptions are actual almost-shortest continuous curves in the ambient
metric, fourPointComparison 1 on the one domain, local complete closed
balls at its points, and dimH(domain)<=n with 1<=n in a nontrivial space.
The conclusion is an actual positive-radius compact ambient closed ball
centered at the prescribed point and contained in that domain.

First, finite separated-set transport from an annulus into a compact closed
chart ball proves total boundedness. Then choose a compact chart patch
center q. If q=p, use that ball. Otherwise choose h<d(q,p)/2 so B(p,h)
lies in the domain, set a=d(q,p)-h, D=d(q,p)+h and
t=min(1/2,rho/(2D)). AC32 applies with its exact positive radial factor.
Restrict to a smaller complete closed ball at p; total boundedness and
completeness give compactness. There is no assumed minimizing segment,
source compactness, family-uniform radius or distant-anchor comparison.
