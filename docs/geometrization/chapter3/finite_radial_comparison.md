# AC30: exact finite radial comparison at curvature minus one

Eight public theorems in four new leaves prove AC30. Four are pure sinh
convexity/scaling estimates. Two derive the hyperbolic weighted-side
inequality and shortening-angle monotonicity directly from four-point
comparison. A purely real cosine-law theorem gives the model lower bound,
and the final metric theorem assembles these on the original five points.

With q,x,y,u,v all in one comparison domain Omega, 0<r=dist(q,x)<=D,
0<s=dist(q,y)<=D, and 0<t<1, assume exactly dist(q,u)=tr,
dist(u,x)=(1-t)r and the analogous two equalities for v,y. Then
(tD/sinh D)*dist(x,y)<=dist(u,v). The constant is exactly blueprint207A's
lambda, positive because t,D>0. No minimizing paths, length property,
local completeness, compactness, tangent or Euclidean realization is
assumed. Omega can be just the finite set of the five supplied points.
The theorem permits x=y and u=v, and the side lemma explicitly handles
z=u; endpoint coincidences are not silently discarded.

The model proof uses the half-angle cosine-law identity and convexity of
sinh on the nonnegative reals. Convexity gives both the radial lower bound
lambda*sinh(h)<=sinh(th) for 0<=h<=D and the contraction upper bound
sinh(lambda*c/2)<=lambda*sinh(c/2). This is equivalent to the blueprint's
arsinh concavity step and avoids introducing a chosen model triangle or
unproved realization. The pure model theorem permits endpoint t=0 or t=1
and zero radial sides; the metric theorem retains AC30's strict hypotheses.
