# Exact equal-radius angle margin: AC68

Five public theorems and one definition in two leaves prove the full AC68 margin and signed configuration. The exact margin is

mu(r,alpha) = min {2r(1-cos(alpha/2)), sqrt(2)r-2r sin(pi/4-alpha/2)}.

It is positive for r>0 and 0<alpha<=1. An equal-leg Euclidean comparison triangle with c in [0,2r] has angle exactly 2arcsin(c/(2r)), including both degenerate endpoints. If its opposite chord differs from 2r by at most D<mu, its angle is strictly greater than pi-alpha and c>0. If the chord differs from sqrt(2)r by at most D<mu, the corresponding bounds are pi/2-alpha and c>0. The scalar error premise already implies D>=0, so that redundant hypothesis is omitted.
