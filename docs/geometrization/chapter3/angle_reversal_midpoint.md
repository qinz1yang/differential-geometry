# AC24–AC25: reversal and balanced almost midpoints

Ten public theorems in five new leaves prove AC24 and AC25 with the exact
blueprint constants. The cosine inverse estimate covers both endpoints of
[0,pi], including equality, through a quadratic bound and its square-root
consumer. Two AC19 applications give comparison-angle reversal with
omega=pi*sqrt(K*s). Four-point comparison on the same Omega at BOTH endpoints
then gives the lower complement pi-delta-2*omega and upper pi+delta.
Positive anchor-distance bounds at both endpoints are explicit. This is not
the stronger single-center formulation of the source strainer lemma.

The balanced curve lemma uses a zero of the difference of endpoint distances
and the actual curve variation. It retains the parameter on the original
curve and allows coincident endpoints; its multiplicative-excess consumer
requires positive endpoint distance. Hyperbolic mean-value estimates and the
actual model cosine law then prove the strict angle bound with
nu=min(1/2,(1-cos(tau))/(8*cosh(2))). For 0<L<=1 and 0<tau<=1 the same point z
has d(x,z)=d(y,z)=s, L/2<=s<(1+nu)L/2<=3L/4 and angle_z(x,y)>pi-tau.
No curvature, completeness, minimizing path or local compactness is used
for AC25. The scalar angle lemma is slightly more general: tau has no upper
bound when its excess budget is supplied separately.
