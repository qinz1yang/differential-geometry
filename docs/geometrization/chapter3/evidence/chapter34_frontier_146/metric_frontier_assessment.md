## The remaining binding obligations, stated without expanding them

1. **MC17 and related MC01/MC10 normalization:** a stabilized inherited Riemannian metric/length/complete-connected-carrier interface must turn tensor estimates into curve-length estimates, then intrinsic-domain distances, then **ambient** ball distances by an actual no-shortcut/exit or convexity argument. It must independently supply the needed image containment or coverage. A tensor estimate or coordinate convergence alone does not discharge this. The metric endpoint is already `PointedBallApprox.ofBilipschitz`; further aliases would not produce the missing geometry.
2. **MC20:** bind the accepted inherited smooth compactness theorem to the precise KL convention and finite regularity/noncollapse/derivative hypotheses. KL Definition3.4 already includes a GH approximation, so invoking that definition is not a proof that tensor convergence alone produces it. This is not authorization to rebuild Hamilton compactness or probe the changing PC snapshot.
3. **MC18 specialization and MC19 downstream uses:** the metric local-comparison input and positive envelope theorem are established. Supplying them from smooth sectional curvature, volume-scale comparisons and smoothing belongs to the appropriate inherited/later chapter interfaces. The generic contracts do not conceal those hypotheses, and this audit does not invent them as new independent MC lemmas.

These are genuine integration requirements, not claims that the user's reported inherited results are absent. No concrete migration-independent MC theorem implementation is recommended. Any broader remaining frontier must come from the separate actual AC/later-chapter audit or the stabilized release, not from historical missing-work prose.
