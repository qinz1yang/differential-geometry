# Growing-region extraction from the original local hypotheses

Five public theorems in three leaves close the written ALG07 metric scope
and the original-input MC18/AC41 extraction bridge. Complete pointed length
sources, a fixed positive integer dimension bound n, local four-point
comparison at parameter kappa_i>=0 on the growing radius-rho_i regions,
kappa_i->0, rho_i->infinity, and AMBIENT dimension at most n are the inputs.
The source length property is actual arbitrarily short continuous curves.
The local comparison may instead be supplied on each ACTUAL constructed
intrinsic open-ball metric, with positive rho_i and explicit metric/topology
parents. No source properness, supplied charts, compactness, nets, global
comparison, dimension conclusion, or already-existing limit is assumed.

The result constructs ONE type Y with ONE metric, basepoint q and strictly
increasing subsequence. That SAME pointed limit is complete and proper,
has actual minimizing metric segments between all pairs, has Hausdorff
dimension at most n, satisfies global zero-curvature four-point comparison
and the quadratic point-on-side lower comparison, and has strict internal
nets on every closed R-ball for every0<delta<=1 with cardinality at most

    (2+16*L_n^2*sqrt(n)*sinh(2*(R+1)))^n * delta^(-n).

This is EXACTLY AC40's C_R*delta^(-n), with the original dimension-only L_n.
The source nets retain the exact AC08 natural ceiling bound. The generic
new ceiling-input extraction theorem likewise retains (2+4*B(R+1))^n and
all geometry conclusions on one completed distance-matrix limit.

The quantified tail proof fixes R and epsilon FIRST, then uses rho_i>256R
and kappa_i<1. It restricts the original local and ambient-dimension data
to this controlled ball and invokes the proved varying-curvature covering
producer. A separate tail uses the original kappa_i and finite dimension
to produce ambient comparison on each fixed ball. Neither tail introduces
source properness or a single index that works for all radii/accuracies.
The accepted eventual-net construction produces the limit; the SAME
subsequence transports the ceiling bounds, local comparisons and source
length curves. Existing finite-configuration angle convergence and dimension
transfer complete the proof. The intrinsic consumer translates local
neighborhoods at the original radius rho_i, not an assumed smaller-ball
intrinsic premise. Kappa_i=0 is allowed by the proved zero branch; this is
a harmless strengthening of the positive sequence in the source statement.
