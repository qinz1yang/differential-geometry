## Statements and source checks

- **LC26:** the canonical cosine estimate assumes nonnegative curvature parameter kappa, r>0, 0<=mu<=1, r<=ell<=(1+mu)r and kappa*r<=1/3. It proves 0<=1+cos(Theta)<=6mu for the actual curvature magnitude kappa^2 and opposite side 2r. Zero curvature and mu=0 are included. The angle consequence works for any tau>=0 satisfying the same strict cosine budget. The Blueprint's r<=b and kappa*b<=1/3 imply this weaker natural radius hypothesis. No Toponogov or Riemannian premise is needed.
- **LC74:** two distinct unbounded connected components outside the ORIGINAL compact K, in a proper space with actual minimizing segments, give an isometric map from the entire real line with its value at zero IN K. The proof constructs arbitrarily long centered segments meeting K. A separate reusable compact-base isometry theorem takes limits of maps with asymptotically vanishing local distance distortion; its domain need not be proper or separable, and the centers need not be fixed. The ultrafilter proof is an alternative to the Blueprint's diagonal proof.
- **LC75 product topology:** for a preconnected unbounded metric Y and any bounded subset K of the max-metric product real x Y, any two unbounded components of its complement coincide. Horizontal and vertical connected slices prove this even without path connectedness or closedness of K. A bi-Lipschitz homeomorphism transfers this to the actual WithLp 2 product. An actual product isometry plus two unbounded components therefore bounds its ORIGINAL transverse factor.
- **LC75 splitting:** the checked Alexandrov line splitting applied to the actual LC74 line gives a compact nonnegative geodesic factor and an onto product isometry. The marked zero slice still meets K. No finite Hausdorff-dimension bound or Riemannian category is assumed.
- **LC75 target identity:** the already checked product-distance estimate supplies explicit pointed maps from globally rescaled E x F to E, with map c*(x-p) and distortion bounded by c*diam(F). Here E can be any complete real normed vector space and F merely bounded. This differs from the earlier ScaledProductCollapse module, which rescales only the transverse metric. Source isometry transport then shows every supplied proper pointed limit of the same globally rescaled product is pointed isometric to E. The multiple-ended source specialization identifies the ORIGINAL supplied C with (real,0). It accepts actual KL maps to that fixed C for all sufficiently large real scales; a fixed-error convergence criterion and the sequence R=n+1 connect those maps to pointed-limit uniqueness. Cone existence is not assumed as a consequence of compactness; the target and maps are supplied, exactly as in LC21/LC75. No replacement cone is chosen.
