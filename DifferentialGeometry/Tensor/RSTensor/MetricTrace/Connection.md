# Connection

## Arbitrary-valence tensor norms

`normSq0S_smooth` proves that the intrinsic squared norm of any smooth
covariant tensor field has smooth spatial dependence. The proof uses the
coordinate-frame expansion of `normSq0S`, smooth inverse-metric components,
and smooth evaluation of the tensor field on coordinate-frame slots.

This generalizes the previously available rank-two result
`normSq02_smooth`. It is the static regularity input needed to realize the
gradient, Hessian, and rough Laplacian of curvature-tower norms.

Focused verification passes. No new analytic or geometric assumption is
introduced.
