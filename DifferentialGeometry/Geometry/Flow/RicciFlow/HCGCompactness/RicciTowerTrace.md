# RicciTowerTrace

## Result

The file identifies the moving Ricci tower as the first-two-slot trace of the
same-order Riemann tower. `ricTower_normSq_le` then bounds its squared norm by
the explicit finite-dimensional trace factor. The realization and
`nablaKRm04Field = iterCov` bridges are proved rather than assumed.
