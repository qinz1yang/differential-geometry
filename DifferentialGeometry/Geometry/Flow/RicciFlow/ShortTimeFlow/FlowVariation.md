## Role in forward uniqueness

This removes the DeTurck-specific restriction from the moving-Jacobian
variation argument.  It is the common input for both signs of the pullback
metric derivative and therefore for the reverse Ricci-to-DeTurck gauge
identity.  It does not construct the harmonic-map heat-flow gauge and does not
by itself advance the endpoint theorem `ricci_flow_forward_unique`, which
remains 0% until its exact Lean statement is proved.

## Remaining frontier

The next consumer is the positive-generator moving-slot identity, followed by
the joint evaluation-form chain rule and Ricci/Lie naturality.  After that
conditional identity, the genuine analytic frontier is short-time existence
and diffeomorphism preservation for the harmonic-map heat equation whose
velocity depends on the pulled-back metric.
