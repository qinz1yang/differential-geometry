# CompleteMetricSegment

Owner Chapter25; claim 73789bcd-7974-466b-9f1e-fe0b6b6e2aef.
SOURCE-ONLY during Chapter23's compiler window. No verification yet.

Realize a whole finite metric segment by one complete intrinsic unit geodesic.
The native short-segment realization gives its initial vector on a prefix
strictly shorter than the exponential radius. At every later parameter the
two metric sublengths add to the total distance, so the checked equality
triangle continuation theorem extends that same vector to the point.

This removes a short-radius restriction from the local realization and is
needed to realize the entire horn arms after compact-family completion. No
smoothness of the original metric segment is assumed, no shortness is
assumed for its full length, and no comparison curvature is used.
