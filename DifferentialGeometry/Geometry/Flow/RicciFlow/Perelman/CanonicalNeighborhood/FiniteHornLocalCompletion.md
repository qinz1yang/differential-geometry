This is the local bridge needed before applying complete-manifold geodesic
tools to a metric end-ray. A compact subradial ball admits a dominating
complete smooth metric agreeing near it. Recenter the native distance-agreement
lemma at each point of a smaller ball: its larger closed ball remains in the
agreement region, so all pairs in the smaller ball retain their actual distances.
The neighborhood, complete metric, domination and agreement are returned as data.

This does not make the original horn complete, or preserve nonnegative curvature
outside the agreement region. It cannot justify the global complete Toponogov
theorem. Local smoothness/identification of metric segments and ray uniqueness
are still required; this producer supplies a legitimate common complete metric
for those local arguments without assuming their conclusions.
