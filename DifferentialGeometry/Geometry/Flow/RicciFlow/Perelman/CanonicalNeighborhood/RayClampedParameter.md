# RayClampedParameter

Owner Chapter25; four-file claim acbb9f06-0df5-4b51-b66d-0b0bc9bf8f3f.
SOURCE-ONLY during Chapter23's window. No verification claim yet.

The actual reparametrization for an arm of length L approximating a ray only
up to radius r is L*(min(s,r)/r). It is monotone, remains on the whole arm,
and differs from s by at most abs(L-r)+(R-r) on 0<=s<=R, r<=R. The ray's
distance identity then adds at most R-r to the known inner approximation
error. This explicit uniform control covers the terminal case hi=a.length
without assuming an outward extension or strict parameter monotonicity.
