# GreatCircle

## Purpose

This module supplies the native round-sphere great-circle producer needed by
the positive constant-curvature classification route.

## Route

The curve is the codomain restriction of
`t ↦ cos t • p + sin t • v`.  Its velocity is identified after applying the
sphere inclusion differential.  The velocity field is built from smooth
gradients of the ambient coordinate functions, so the existing covariant
chain rule applies without a new section-along-curve assumption.  The
round-sphere projection connection reduces its covariant derivative to the
ambient skew operator generated by `p` and `v`; its square is the negative
identity on the great-circle plane, hence its tangential projection vanishes.
