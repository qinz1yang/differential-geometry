# Initial chart interval

## Status

`exists_chart_head` is verified.  It extracts a positive closed initial
subinterval of a continuous real-parameter curve that remains in any chart
source containing the initial point.  The statement needs only continuity on
the original compact interval and no manifold smoothness or compactness.

## Role

The result supports corner-localization arguments: after a piecewise regular
curve reaches a shared node, a short head of the next piece can be represented
in the preceding chart.  The remaining tail can then be kept unchanged in a
global comparison and cancelled.
