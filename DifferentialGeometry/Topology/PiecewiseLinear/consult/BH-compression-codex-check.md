# BH case (b): Codex source check of digest BK

Scope: due diligence only. No frozen statement or proof module was changed. The reviewer's geometric fixture is not in this checkout, so the fixture's 25 cut-frame, 14 graph-frame and ten invariant clauses were not machine checked here.

- `Skeleton/Section34Compact.lean` defines `Section34CompactFaceBallInvariants` with field 4 `fbl s ∩ fbl s' ⊆ interior (⋃ w, tgtV w)` for distinct faces. Enlarging a face ball by filling a pocket can violate that field even when the original balls satisfied it. The compression predicate only asserts a PL disk `Dj`, its rim in the current face-ball boundary, disjointness from edge disks, and disjointness of `Dj \ Jd` from other *old* face balls. It does not constrain the new filled region against another old face ball.
- Graph-frame field 8 places the image of the simplex rim inside the face torus. Its last field gives `Section34CompactExterior` for the original image of the simplex body. Neither clause mentions a modified face ball, so neither alone excludes the proposed pocket-finger position. The face-ball invariant's last field does constrain exterior components for the current balls; the reviewer says the full pre-surgery fixture satisfies it, but no Lean fixture is available to replay.
- The cut frame gives at least one incident tetrahedron for each face. The two-tetrahedra escape step needs the manifold incidence fact for an interior face, identification of the two tetrahedra in the cut/graph frame, and the exterior-component argument for a vertex nonincident to at least one of them. Those implications have not been compiled as one theorem.
- The controlled through-tube route needs separate sub-leaves: a thin PL 2-handle giving a shell with two PL sphere boundaries; a proper PL arc through that shell inside `interior N` and outside the possibly non-PL rim and closed obstacles; a small regular neighbourhood of the arc whose complement in the shell is a PL 3-ball; preservation of every other face ball and all ten invariants; exact trace loss by one and no crossing-count increase. An arbitrary ambient arc is too weak for the count conclusion.
