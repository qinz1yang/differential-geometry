## Preferred Lemma 2 construction

Use the proposed upstairs surgery as the first implementation route. Reuse
existing L1, boundary-word alternatives, supported wedge deformations and
strict-descent results where their actual hypotheses match. Keep a fallback
to the existing downstairs construction if a concrete upstairs obligation
turns out more expensive; do not maintain two duplicate proof developments.
This choice is a source-based architecture decision, not a completed proof
or a new verification of a particular edition of Hempel.

For a fixed original double cover p and an upstairs embedded disk D, the next
consumer should obtain its replacement D' from the actual branch geometry.
It must establish all of the following, rather than accept them as inputs:

- An actual PL deck involution on the needed ambient neighborhood, with
  p composed with the involution equal to p, and its relation to the existing
  partner involution on the disk's double-point preimage.
- The appropriate paired arcs or invariant closed-branch case, with disjoint
  supported neighborhoods, compatible full-branch charts, ordered normal
  directions, and endpoint data. Pointwise charts are insufficient.
- A properly embedded replacement disk in the same covering manifold. Its
  whole image and boundary trace remain in the required neighborhoods. The
  applicable boundary-word alternative proves avoidance of the original
  normal subgroup; avoidance is not automatic for a newly chosen boundary.
- An exact identity or a sufficient injective correspondence for the actual
  intersections of D' with its deck image. Two named embedded disks bound
  projected fiber multiplicity, but do not imply transverse crossings or
  absence of newly created intersection branches.
- Strict decrease for the actual normal-disk branch measure, including the
  relation between upstairs components and downstairs deck orbits. A genuine
  closed-branch case needs its own incidence proof; it is not certified by
  calling it the same wedge mechanism.
- One application of the canonical projection producer to the same D',
  followed by the remaining normal-crossing/triangulation and tower-return
  obligations. Do not rebuild projection bookkeeping independently per case.

The projection route removes arbitrary raw-NormalSystem normalization from
the primary path. It does not remove relative general position for the actual
projected disk, global branch geometry, boundary alternatives or strict descent.
